Why URSO exists.

We think stout has been stuck in winter for too long. Fruit drinks get the sunshine; stout gets the cold. We wanted one drink that does both.

URSO is rooted in Warwickshire, and so is the bear. He carries his keg wherever the party is, in any season.

The name comes from ursus, the Latin for bear.
