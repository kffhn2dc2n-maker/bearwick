    Our Warwick story.

    Everyone loves stout. Plenty of people also find it too heavy, too bitter, too filling. Meanwhile, modern flavoured beers proved that people will happily try beer in new ways.

    So we asked: what if stout could be as easy to drink as a lager? We have roots in Warwick, England. That gave us the name, and a bear.
